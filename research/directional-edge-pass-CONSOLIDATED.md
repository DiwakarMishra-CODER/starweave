# 2. RULINGS APPLIED

## a) Cover-derived edges → **DO NOT ENCODE**

Affects: `Big Thief → Townes Van Zandt` · `Jeff Buckley → Leonard Cohen` · `Waxahatchee → Silver Jews` · `Bonnie Prince Billy → Cat Power`

**Reasoning.** The exclusion has to hold categorically or it collapses. Covers appeared constantly in this research — Iggy on the VU tribute, Crutchfield covering Silver Jews, Oldham covering Cat Power, Buckley covering Bad Brains and Cohen, Buck Meek covering Van Zandt. Nearly every cover is framed as tribute by *someone*, so "tribute framing promotes it" would readmit the whole category.

On the Buck Meek case specifically: "The Influences" is the **publication's** editorial frame, not the artist's claim. That is structurally identical to the Apple Music playlist rule your brief already sets — commentary written around an artefact is downstream of the artefact, not evidence for it.

**But keep them.** See §4.

## b) `Snail Mail → Duster` and `Alex G → Duster` → **hold at 0.60, do not upgrade**

Two dedicated searches into each artist's own interviews found **no Duster mention in either**. Both artists talk extensively about influences. That leaves one syndicated AllMusic sentence carrying both edges, with Obscure Sound's "expressed admiration" (fandom) and Stereogum's co-billing note as the only company.

Consider that the AllMusic sentence may be a critic's inference from the reunion co-billing and the TikTok resurgence narrative rather than a claim about influence. **Duster's low in-degree may be substantially correct.**

## c) `Bonnie Prince Billy → Big Black` → **0.70 → 0.60**

Attempting the book turned up a second review that complicates the first. Full Stop describes Oldham's rise in Louisville as "first and foremost as a fan," and notes the Big Black connection runs through his **brothers Paul and Ned**, who played in bands with Steve Albini while Will was doing theatre. Time Out framed the same material as musical education via gigs.

Two reviews of one book, disagreeing on education vs. fandom-plus-family-proximity. Hüsker Dü stays at 0.70 (no brother-shaped complication). **The book remains the only thing that settles either.**

## d) `Waxahatchee → Superchunk` → **DO NOT ENCODE**

"I have been a Superchunk fan for a long time" is fandom by your own standard, and it was said to **Mac McCaughan — simultaneously Superchunk's frontman and her label boss at Merge.** Two contamination vectors, no influence language. Worth one search in a non-Merge setting; not worth encoding on this.

# 12. METHOD NOTES FOR FUTURE PASSES

**1. Never search "who cites X." Search the suspected descendant's own interviews.**
Nine cases to zero. Every per-descendant search produced first-person material on the first try — Fiona Apple, Bon Iver, Jeff Buckley, Hum, Snail Mail, Waxahatchee, Japanese Breakfast. Every root-outward downstream search produced critic resemblance or nothing — Duster, Bark Psychosis, Beach House, Deerhunter, Sufjan, Sleater-Kinney, Pinback, Fleet Foxes, Bonnie Prince Billy.

**2. Upstream is cheap; downstream is expensive.**
Upstream averaged one search for 2–5 edges (Big Thief 4, Jeff Buckley 5, The Fall 4, Snail Mail 6). Downstream averaged 1–2 searches for 0–2. The direction has a bounded answer only one way round.

**3. Three formats carry almost everything.**
The Quietus's *Baker's Dozen* · gear-and-technique interviews · book-length interviews. Hayward on Faust's mixing system, Moreno on Hum's tone, Chelmis on pedal steel, Bridgers on four-track tape, Jordan on classical guitar training. **Not one of 64 edges came from a query containing the word "influence."**

**4. Anchor to album titles, not artist names.**
Roster artists with common-word names — Beck, Wire, Hum, Low, Women, Suicide, Sparks, Faust, Codeine — will not resolve on the name alone.

**5. The roster's closure produces genuine zeroes.**
Hum→Deftones, Snail Mail→Mary Timony, Bonnie Prince Billy→Sally Timms, Ethel Cain→Nicole Dollanganger, Olivia Tremor Control→John Cage. For several artists a low count is the *correct* value against 290 closed nodes. Marking those as verified rather than unresearched would make the graph more honest and stop wasted passes.

**6. Rejection density predicts hazard.**
